# COMPASS Interview Preparation Prompt

```text
Generate COMPASS interview preparation notes using the user's current instruction, verified Intake claim ledger and do-not-claim list, and latest approved canonical career record first. Use the target role, job description, recruiter request, current COMPASS analysis, and interview context as preparation inputs only.

Required framework files:
- VERSION.md
- COMPASS_Current.md
- rules/00-operating-principles.md
- rules/01-analysis-workflow.md
- rules/04-truthguard.md
- rules/06-artifact-rules.md
- rules/20-professional-effectiveness-evidence.md

Treat this prompt as a workflow launcher, not as an independent source of interview-preparation, artifact, TruthGuard, professional-effectiveness, source-priority, or no-fabrication rules.

Use the strict interview preparation notes template in rules/06-artifact-rules.md unless I explicitly request a different format.

Apply rules/20-professional-effectiveness-evidence.md to explicit and implicit behavioral hiring signals. Build an evidence-backed story bank for the most material capabilities, including likely question, approved situation, candidate action or decision, constraint or tradeoff, stakeholder or system boundary, verified consequence or outcome, and claim-depth/do-not-claim boundary. Prefer stories that naturally demonstrate multiple connected capabilities without splicing unrelated experiences together.

Keep preparation notes source-grounded. Flag gaps and TruthGuard cautions instead of converting unverified target requirements into candidate claims.
```
